# Elist_Analysis
Performance Report 
# Overview
Founded in 2018, **Elist is an e-commerce company that sells popular electronics products** and has since expanded to a global customer base. Like most e-commerce companies, Elist sells products through its online site as well as through its mobile app. They use a variety of marketing channels to reach customers, including Email campaigns, SEO, and affiliate links. Over the last few years, their more popular products have been products from Apple, Samsung, and ThinkPad.

## Business Questions
**What were the overall trends in sales from 2019-2022?**
**What were Elist's monthly and yearly growth rates?**
**How is the new loyalty program performing? Should we keep using it?**
**What were our refund rates and average order values?**

## North Star Metrics
**Sales trends** - Focusing on key metrics like total sales, number of orders placed, and average order value (AOV) by region, product, and brand.
**Growth rate** - Analyzing growth rates by different dimensions, growth rates in sales and AOV. 
**Product performance** - Analyzing different product categories, market impact, and refund rates to inform strategic product decisions.
**Loyalty program performance** - Evaluating the effectiveness of the company's loyalty program and providing recommendations to maximize customer engagement and retention.
**Regional results** - Evaluating regional demand and product performance within regions to identify areas for improvement.
