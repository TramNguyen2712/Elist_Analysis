<h2 align='center'>Ecommerce Performance Report 2019-2022</h2>  

<div class='Overview'>
  <h1 align='center'>Overview</h1>
  <p>Founded in 2018, <strong>Elist is an e-commerce company that sells popular electronics products</strong> and has since expanded to a global customer base. Like most e-commerce companies, Elist sells products through its website as well as its mobile app. They use a variety of marketing channels to reach customers, including Email campaigns, SEO, and affiliate links. Over the last few years, their most popular items have been Apple, Samsung, and Lenovo products.</p>
  <p>This report analyzes Elist's sales performance from 2019 to 2022 to identify key trends, assess business growth, and understand changes in customer purchasing behavior. By examining sales volume, average order value (AOV), return rates, and loyalty program effectiveness, the report aims to identify opportunities to increase revenue, improve customer retention, and minimize potential losses from returns. These findings will help Elist make data-driven decisions regarding marketing strategies, product performance, and future business growth.</p>
  
  <h2> North Star Metrics </h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Sales Trends</strong> - Focusing on key metrics like total sales, number of orders placed, and average order value (AOV) by region, product, and brand. </li>
    <li><strong>Growth Rate</strong> - Analyzing growth rates by different dimensions, including sales and AOV.</li>
    <li><strong>Product Performance</strong> - Analyzing different product categories, market impact, and refund rates to inform strategic product decisions.</li>
    <li><strong>Region</strong> - Evaluating regional demand and product performance within regions to identify areas for improvement. </li>
    <li><strong>Loyalty Program Performance</strong> - Evaluating the effectiveness of the company's loyalty program and providing recommendations to maximize customer engagement and retention. </li>
  </ul>
    
  <h2> Summary </h2>
  <p>Elist's sales nearly tripled in 2020 as COVID-19 pushed online shopping online, peaked at $1.25M in December 2020, then declined through 2021 and 2022. However, that was still 28% above 2019.</p>
  <p><strong>Key Insights:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Sales:</strong> 2020 growth came from both more orders (+102%) and AOV (+31%). Since then, AOV returned to its 2019 level, and order volume fell 38% in 2022.</li>
    <li><strong>Seasonality:</strong> December and September were the strongest months. October and February were the weakest every year. Monthly sales peaked in December 2020 and stepped down in early 2021, early 2022, and again in October 2022 ($0.18M, the low of the period).</li>
    <li><strong>Product:</strong> The 27-inch gaming monitor, MacBook Air, and AirPods drive about 84% of sales. The monitor alone grew to about half of sales by late 2022.</li>
    <li><strong>Region:</strong> North America is about half of sales and held up best in 2022. APAC grew the most since 2019, while LATAM tended to shift toward low-price accessories.</li>
    <li><strong>Loyalty:</strong> Loyalty members overtook non-members in early 2021 and now make up 55% of sales, spending $31 more per order ($245 vs $214).</li>
    <li><strong>Refunds:</strong> Laptops are refunded most often (about 1 in 6 orders in 2019-2020), while AirPods and monitors drove refund amounts. The refund data in 2022 was missing.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class='Recommendation'>
  <h1 align='center'>Recommendations</h1>

  <div class='Sales Trends'>
    <h2>Sales</h2>
    <p>Sales usually fell sharply in October, with a 55% decline in October 2022. Introduce October promotions, targeted discounts, or product bundles to maintain demand between September and the holiday season.</p>
    <p>November and December were consistently strong months during 2019-2022. Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of November and December to capture peak holiday demand.</p>

  </div>

  <div class='Product Performance'>
    <h2>Product</h2>
    <p>With three main products, gaming monitor, MacBook Air, and AirPods, maintain sufficient inventory, especially for the monitor, to capture order share gains toward the end of 2022.</p>
    <p>Bundle lower-priced accessories such as charging cables with laptops and monitors to increase basket value.</p>
    <p>Investigate the low sales of Bose Headphones and Apple iPhones before deciding whether to reduce inventory or discontinue products. </p>

  </div>

  <div class='Loyalty Program'>
    <h2>Loyalty Program</h2>
    <p>Expand loyalty benefits by promotions, reward points, and personalized product recommendations.</p>
    <p>Convert repeat non-loyalty customers into loyalty members through sign-up incentives at checkout.</p>

  </div>

  <div class='Region'>
    <h2>Region</h2>
    <p>All regions experienced a similar pandemic-related sales cycle, but LATAM had the biggest sales decline in 2022 at 56%. Prioritize LATAM for further investigation into the sales decline and the shift toward lower-priced products.</p>
    <p>Promote monitors in APAC, where monitor order share increased from 20.8% to 24.66%.</p>
    <p>In NA, use accessories with laptop or monitor bundles to increase revenue per order. </p>

  </div>

  <div class='Refund Rate'>
    <h2>Refund Rate</h2>
    <p>MacBook Air and ThinkPad laptops had the highest refund rate, so investigate the reasons for laptop returns (product defects, shipping damage, and mismatches between product descriptions and customer expectations). </p>
    <p>Work with the data engineering team to investigate missing 2022 refund records.</p>
